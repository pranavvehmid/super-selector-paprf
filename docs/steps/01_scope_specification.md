# Step 1 Scope Specification

**Status:** Approved for progression to Step 2 on 18 November 2026.

## Official deliverables

1. Ordered top-10 batters under pressure.
2. Ordered top-10 bowlers under pressure.
3. Reproducible methodology report that supports both lists.

Batting and bowling are separate ranking problems. Pressure performance must be compared with each player's usual performance.

## Primary analytical population

- Include IPL and T20I records from the supplied historical data.
- Exclude Men's T20 Emerging Teams Asia Cup 2024 and Asia Cup Rising Stars 2025 from the primary ranking population.
- Retain the two excluded competitions for a later, explicitly labelled sensitivity check only.
- Use stable identifiers rather than player names for joins.

## Active Workstream 1 method

- **Planned official method:** Lens A, a transparent pressure-versus-own-baseline statistical rating with later sample-size protection and uncertainty checks.
- **PLS status:** The phrase PLS is treated as a working interpretation of Partial Least Squares, pending confirmation. No PLS model is active and it cannot replace the planned official ranking.

## Boundary with Workstreams 2 and 3

Current RR squad-building needs and RR-specific player-fit recommendations are separate contextual outputs. They can explain or filter candidate recommendations after the primary ranking is frozen, but cannot alter the scores or ordering of the official batter and bowler lists.
