# Audit of Lens A v1 Against the User’s Master Scouting Prompt

## Finding

Lens A v1 is appropriate for the official Round 1 question: reliable player performance under pre-delivery pressure versus the player’s own ordinary baseline. It is not, by itself, a complete professional scouting model. This is a limitation, not a flaw concealed by the final rankings.

## Retained strengths

| Requirement area | Evidence-backed Lens A capability | Why it remains |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure handling | Pre-delivery index, player baseline and shrinkage. | Directly answers the official challenge without leakage. |
| Quantity versus reliability | Minimum opportunities/matches and 60-ball shrinkage prior. | Limits small-sample overstatement. |
| Role separation | Distinct batting and bowling metrics, signs and peer groups. | Avoids generic runs/wickets scoring. |
| Reproducibility | Scripts, versioned CSVs, manifest and validators. | Supports independent checking. |
| Transparency | Ambiguous events held out; sensitivity results disclosed. | Makes uncertainty auditable. |

## Evidence gaps for a broader scouting model

| Dimension | Why it cannot be scored honestly now | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Technical execution | Ball-by-ball outcomes do not show mechanics, range, control or execution quality. | Match video and structured scouting observations. |
| Tactical intelligence | A delivery record does not prove intent, field plan or reading of conditions. | Analyst-coded match/video notes. |
| Adaptability | Supplied fields do not establish venue, pitch or weather difficulty. | Venue/conditions data and sufficient samples. |
| Opposition quality | No verified opponent-strength model is supplied. | Dated strength source and declared adjustment. |
| Recent trajectory | Lens A is not a recency-weighted model. | Dated event chronology and predeclared recency rule. |
| Fielding, fitness, injury | Not in supplied fields; must not be inferred. | Verified public/official evidence. |
| Availability, price and RR fit | No dated source-backed current dataset is supplied. | data/external files with source and retrieved_on. |

## Safeguard and integration plan

No qualitative score will be fabricated just to complete a table. Each future score must state the source, reviewer, scale, uncertainty and formula. Missing evidence is Not assessed—not zero and not a guess.

1. Preserve Lens A v1 as the official pressure-reliability ranking.
2. Build a source-backed candidate-discovery register.
3. Define role-specific rubrics only after evidence types are known.
4. Record technical/tactical statements as cited observations.
5. Add evidence confidence separately from performance scores.
6. Audit country and data coverage before a broader shortlist.
7. Apply RR squad fit only as a Lens C explanation/filter, never as a change to Lens A rank.
