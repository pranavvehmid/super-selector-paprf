# Official Round 1 requirements (facts only)

Source: `Round 1 Problem Statement _ SupeRR Selector Season 2.docx.pdf` (as summarised in `docs/original/`). Keep interpretations out of this file.

- **Challenge:** Identify the Most Reliable Players Under Pressure — a data-driven system measuring how batters and bowlers perform in high-pressure situations vs. their usual performance, ranking those who deliver most consistently when it matters.
- **Pressure definition:** left to the participant; may use required run rate, wickets in hand, balls remaining, innings stage, closeness of contest, combinations, other context. Must be explained, distinguish pressure from ordinary situations, and work for batting and bowling.
- **Data (per PDF):** IPL and T20I, 2022 onwards — ball-by-ball, competitions, teams, players, matches, playing XIs, competition-team-player mappings.
- **Deliverable A (Unstop):** ordered top-10 batters (Rank 1–10) and top-10 bowlers (Rank 1–10); Rank 1 = highest-rated; no repeats within a list; dropdown selections must match the report.
- **Deliverable B (report):** pressure definition; performance measurement (metrics, baselines, rating logic); fair comparisons (sample size, eligibility, context, IPL/T20I use); evidence for the top 10; reliability and limitations. Must be reproducible; rankings must be supported by supplied data, not reputation.
- **Optional:** photos/screenshots or a 30–60 s video; does not affect evaluation.
- **From the event briefing (user-supplied):** explain how each recommended player could strengthen the Rajasthan Royals squad.
